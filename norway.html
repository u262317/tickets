Hours come from listing sites and can change, so confirm on the day. Buy chilled items last; February is cold enough to keep them fine in the car. Loyalty apps (Trumf, Coop, Rema 1000) are mainly for Norwegian residents.

Keeping costs down

  • Self-catering apartment rather than a hotel.
  • Check youth/child lift pass age limits (many discount under 15 or 16).
  • Book flights and accommodation early; half-term prices rise fast.
